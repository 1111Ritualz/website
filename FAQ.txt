Honest answers to questions we hear most (AKA FAQ’s).

“Is this just another bath salt?”
No. Every bath salt on the market is made with essential oils, machine-blended, and produced at scale with no awareness of what it’s touching. Ocean Shield is hand-pounded in a sage-cleansed space while specific mantras play. It uses real herbs, whole lavender buds, not lavender extract, because we believe the intelligence of the plant exists in the whole material. It is made differently because it is for a different purpose.
“I don’t have a bathtub. Can I still use it?”
Yes, and most of our users don’t. The bucket method (two to three  tablespoons dissolved in warm water, poured from head to toe after your shower) is the traditional method and equally effective. The mug method (one to two tablespoon in a mug of warm water poured at the end of your shower) is the most common daily practice. You do not need a bathtub.
“How long will one jar last?”
One jar contains enough for 30 to 45 daily uses depending on your method and amount. At daily use, approximately one to one and a half months per jar.

Both. The energetic body is documented in Ayurvedic medicine across 3,000 years of recorded practice. We hold both the science and the tradition with equal respect. You are welcome to bring whichever lens you prefer.
